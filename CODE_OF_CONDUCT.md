# Code of Conduct

We want Awesome LoomLoom to be a respectful, practical place to share work and improve the ecosystem.

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect different experience levels, and focus feedback on the project or contribution. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, and deceptive promotion are not welcome.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or limit participation when needed to protect the community.
